Connecting...

Wi-Fi

The watch joins your home network for photos, maps and the assistant. After it joins, reach this page at http://orbit.local.

Photos

Photos are cropped to a circle and resized for the watch before upload. They appear in the Photos app, the photo watch face and the always-on screen.

Custom watch faces

Build up to six custom faces for the Custom watch face slot. Tap the Custom face on the watch to cycle between them.

Keys

Fourteen buttons across two pages. The first of each page sits in the middle. Leave a label empty to hide a button.

Assistant

The key is stored on the watch and is never shown again. Usage is billed to your OpenAI account.

Camera

On the LUMIX: Wi-Fi > Wi-Fi Function > New Connection > Remote Shooting & View > Direct. The camera shows its network name and password. The watch leaves your home Wi-Fi while the Camera app is open.

Maps

Downloads about 90 map tiles around the point and remembers it for Sun Times. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Update

First press Allow update on the watch (Settings > System), then choose orbit.bin. If the new version fails to start, the watch goes back to this one.
